There it is, somewhere below this post, the PAX Schedule. Look it over. What do you think? Is there anything at all that interests you?

I’ll throw out the stuff that I really want to see:

On Friday, I would like to see the KEYNOTE, and the Penny Arcade Q&A Panel. Then, I don’t know, not much else except the Jonathan Coulton concert and maybe stopping by the rock band freeplay?

On Saturday, maybe the Penny Arcade “Make A Strip?” I’m torn because “The History of Harmonix” is at the same time. I think I’d rather hit the Make a Strip, though, only because I want to see the FALLOUT 3 presentation right after it. Maybe one of the D&D panels right after that, or check out the Guitar Hero tournament? That’s pretty much it for Saturday. We could check out the expo hall a little bit during the day, and later maybe see one of the movies or hit one of the freeplays, or explore the city.

On Sunday, it’s kind of the same deal. I really want to see the DRAGON AGE presentation at 1:30, so I might want to hit the Penny Arcade/Family Feud things in between. I don’t know if you want to see SPORE at all.

So, I think that actually does leave us some good time to explore the city a little bit. It wouldn’t hurt to make some time to figure out if there’s anything around there you’d be interested in checking out.
