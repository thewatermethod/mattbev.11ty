> **Abandoned Detroit House is Transformed with 36,000 Flowers**
> 
> In November 2014, florist [Lisa Waud](http://potandbox.com/) bought this abandoned, crumbling duplex in Detroit Michigan. Winning the bid at $250, Waud had not even seen the home. Her vision was to transform the location into an art installation called Flower House.
> 
> To ornate the home, Waud invited florists Michigan, Ohio, New York and Canada to install different flower installations in every room.
