Brew Day 3/14

Brewed a chocolate stout yesterday, erring a bit on the imperial side. However, I wasn’t paying attention to my pre-boil volume and produced too much wort; also, I do a full wort boil on the stovetop, so my boil sucks… I definitely need to redo my math. I ended up with almost 8 gallons of wort after two hours of boiling. The gravity ended up being 1.060, where it probably should have been closer to 1.090. Oh well.

10 lbs Maris Otter

4 lbs Belgian Pale Ale

.5 lbs English Roasted Barley

.5 English Chocolate Malt

.5 Weyermann Chocolate Wheat

2 oz First Gold at 60 Min

1 oz First Gold at 30 Min

1 lbs Light Brown Sugar at 30 min

1 oz Fuggles at 15 min

8 oz Cocoa Powder at 15 min

1 oz Fuggles at Flameout

2 Packages Wyeast 1028
