> GEHORSAMKEIT
> 
> _\[noun\]_
> 
> obedience; dutifulness; complying or willing to comply with orders or requests; submissive to another’s will; to conform.
> 
> _Etymology_: German, _gehorsam_ (obedient) +- _keit_ (suffix to convert an adjective into a noun).
