> > So I did this really silly thing.
> > 
> > I have a very “Instagram glam” kind of apartment going on, and not a lot of “nerdy” stuff is made in that kind of look. Blush pink, gold, succulents, and minimalist aesthetic is not really high priority for people with the same interests as me. So when I had to pick out some art to put over my bed, I went through all the minimalist city/country maps I could find. I love Paris, but even that didn’t feel right. Then I decided to make my own based on Ravenloft, the thing I love most in all of gaming.
> > 
> > I based it on the Curse of Strahd map. I have the actual canon date that Strahd settled the land up top, landmarks and rivers and roads according to the book. I added on rough population estimations based on the declining population after many places were left in ruins. Also details about the climate, and a big blurb about life in Barovia on the left.
> > 
> > I really like it. It was made to work on a 24 x 36 horizontal poster. 
> > 
> > (If anyone wants the full size image, I’ll be happy to send it either in png or pdf your way. I don’t think I’ll put this one up as a print in my etsy store even though I considered it briefly.)
>
