> I’m creating a Pulp Call of Cthulhu campaign, and I’m looking to literally populate it with Pulp action heroes, in the same mode as [this](http://www.cthulhudice.com/batman-indiana-jones-james-bond-tom-riddle-doctor-pulp-cthulhu/). I figured I’d translate a handful of characters to the Call of Cthulhu rules and then present them to my players and let them choose. I’m looking to avoid “superheroes” but pretty much any character is fair game.
> 
> I’ve started with the “big three.”
