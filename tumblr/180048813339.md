> I’ve been doing more writing on my interminable campaign notes document, and here’s my main overarching threat. He’s an abbot possessed by Orcus, who travels into the wastes, engendering madness, raising weird undead and summoning demons from the earth by mere prescence alone. I like the idea of a villain who doesn’t do much except wander around the desert arguing with himself, but still has to be dealt with by the time he finally resolves his disparate personalities and ruins everything.
