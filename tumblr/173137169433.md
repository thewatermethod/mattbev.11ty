> Early Thanos concept drawing by Jim Starlin and Rich Buckler, 1971. 
> 
> From the auction listing:  
> “This is probably one of the first concept drawings of Thanos I ever did,“ Starlin said… the design was done “long before I started working at Marvel… Jack Kirby’s Metron is clearly the more dominant influence in this character’s look. Not Darkseid. Both \[Darkseid and Thanos\] started off much smaller than they originally became. This was one of the drawings I had in my portfolio when I was hired by Marvel. It was later inked by Rich Buckler.”
