> The Symbolist movement refers not to the literal practice of using one thing to represent another, but to an aesthetic, literary and artistic movement of the nineteenth century. The movement has close ties to the Romantic movement, which spawned _Frankenstein_ (the novel) as well as Polidori’s _The Vampyr_, which can be seen as a sort of precursor to Dracula.
