> ‘John Carpenter Classics’ by [Matt Ferguson](https://www.cakesandcomics.com/), artwork for 4k restorations of four classic films directed by John Carpenter; ‘The Fog’, ‘Escape From New York’, ‘Prince Of Darkness’ and 'They Live’.  
>   
> All four brand new 4k restorations were done using the original camera negatives, with the colour grading approved for the new restorations and UHD versions by the films’ Cinematographers: Gary B Kibbe and Dean Cundey.  
>   
> 'The Fog’ will receive its restoration premiere at [The London Film Festival](https://whatson.bfi.org.uk/lff/Online/default.asp) on October 15. From October 26, 'The Live’ and 'Prince Of Darkness’ will screen as a precursor to the big Halloween one-night special of 'The Fog’, and 'Escape From Escape From New York’ will be screened for one day only on November 22.  
>   
> The new 4k restorations will also be released across Home Entertainment platforms including UHD for the very first time, with new brand new and ‘best of’extras material, and a special 4-disc Collector’s Edition that will also include a copy of the film’s seminal soundtrack composed and performed by Carpenter himself.  
> 
> Available to pre-order Friday September 7 at 6pm UK from the [Zavvi website](http://tidd.ly/13598c54).
