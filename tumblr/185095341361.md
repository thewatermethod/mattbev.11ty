> _[The Ruins of Undermountain](http://oldschoolfrp.tumblr.com/tagged/the%20ruins%20of%20undermountain)_ (1991) included 4 big poster-sized maps, each 21″x31″, for levels 1, 2, and 3 (north and south) of Halaster’s dungeons below The Yawning Portal.  Only certain portions were keyed and described in the books, with the rest left for the DM to expand as desired.
