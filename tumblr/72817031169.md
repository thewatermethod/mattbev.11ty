I’m going to try and write 52 book reviews this year. Approximately one a week. I think this is entirely doable if I cheat and allow myself to write about “graphic novels.”

I’m writing two reviews this week. Without further ado:

![Alphonse Mucha Masterworks](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/9e98771daed4b11ecb2040798733d7a2e50e639e05a0966419cb8b0d5dc40fa9.jpg "Alphonse Mucha Masterworks")

1/52

[Alphonse Mucha Masterworks](http://www.amazon.com/Alphonse-Mucha-Masterworks-Rosalind-Ormiston/dp/1844517306/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1389313961&sr=8-1&keywords=mucha+masterworks "Alphonse Mucha Masterworks on Amazon") by Rosalind Ormiston

A worthwhile endeavor.The text is fairly slight.The first half of the book offers a short biography of Mucha, who led an interesting enough life. 

The real draw here is the high quality reproductions of Mucha’s art. In this respect, the book does not disappoint.

His style is so [widely imitated](http://www.pinterest.com/thewatermethod/art-mucha-mucha-pastiche/) that you’ll certainly recognize his most famous pieces. 

I doubt that I’ll refer to the text again, not when it contains no more than his [wikipedia](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphonse_Mucha) page does. I suspect that I’ll be opening it up for years to come anyway. I find Mucha’s art that compelling.
