Douglas Rushkoff is the newest guest blogger on Boing Boing. Previously, I knew of him primarily as the writer of the DC/Vertigo comic TESTAMENT, but I’d heard the name in conjunction with the term ‘futurist’ and even, I think, in some of the occult stuff I’ve read.

Anyway, as his introductory post for Boing Boing, he writes a simple little piece which lays out the liberal and conservative mindset in a way I’ve never really thought of before, tying in the resurgent 'maker’ trend, and the culture wars.

I enjoyed the terms he put it in.
