> NIGHT OF THE EAGLE is drier than the marketing and poster would have you believe, but I still found it interesting and engaging.
> 
> It’s kind of hard to talk about the movie without giving away too much (I don’t think the gifs really give you too much of an idea of how anything happens in the movie or what exactly is going on necessarily. If you’re concerned about spoilers, you should always be unfollowing or blacklisting the title), but It’s about a woman that practices a sort of dark magic that she uses to protect her husband and household from potentially harmful entities. Her husband is a professor and big time skeptic, so there’s a conflict that arises between them that drives the rest of the movie.
> 
> I’m never sure what to expect with older movies, but more often than not I am pleasantly surprised. Some others I really enjoyed off the top of my head are DEMENTIA 13, THE INNOCENTS, THE PHANTOM CARRIAGE, DEMENTIA aka DAUGHTER OF HORROR, MURDERS IN THE ZOO, THE BAD SEED, CARNIVAL OF SOULS, DIABOLIQUE, and the original PHANTOM OF THE OPERA
