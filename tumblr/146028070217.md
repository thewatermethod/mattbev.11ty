> ATTENTION: URBAN ORACLES, HEX HUNX, STREET STREGHE

> SUBJ: LAST STOP 666 MILES FOR ART, PATCHES, RESTROOMS  
> 
> [This afternoon we have listed a small selection of \[PROBLEM GLYPHS\] merchandise from our back stock on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/elizagauger?ref=hdr_shop_menu).
> 
> These items include a handful of matted Problem Glyph prints.  Printed on Pro Art 140# (300 gsm) acid-free cold press watercolor paper, these 4 x 6" (15.24 x 10.16cm) prints are signed in unreproduceable gold ink by artist, Eliza Guager. These prints are matted and suitable for framing or can be hung as they are.
> 
> We have also released small quantities of every patch we have ever produced. In most cases, less than 10 are available of each. Patches include: [\[MAY WE BE MARTYRED IN SPACE\]](https://www.etsy.com/listing/246831294/problem-glyphs-martyred-in-space?ref=shop_home_active_11), [\[BOG WITCH\]](https://www.etsy.com/listing/246828990/problem-glyphs-bog-witch-screenprinted?ref=shop_home_active_12), [\[THE JARL\]](https://www.etsy.com/listing/266886556/problem-glyphs-the-jarl-screenprinted?ref=shop_home_active_14), [\[THERE IS LIFE ON MARS\]](https://www.etsy.com/listing/272068172/problem-glyphs-there-is-life-on-mars?ref=shop_home_active_9), [\[EVERY BREATH IS VENGEANCE\]](https://www.etsy.com/listing/272067782/problem-glyphs-every-breath-is-vengeance?ref=shop_home_active_8), [\[TRUST NO ONE\]](https://www.etsy.com/listing/272068712/problem-glyphs-trust-no-one?ref=shop_home_active_10), and [\[WARD\]](https://www.etsy.com/listing/266887080/problem-glyphs-ward-screenprinted-canvas?ref=shop_home_active_13).
> 
> Once these patches and prints are sold out they will not return for sale until after the upcoming [Problem Glyphs art book Kickstarter, beginning May 16th](https://www.etsy.com/listing/266887080/problem-glyphs-ward-screenprinted-canvas?ref=shop_home_active_13). Over 100 8"x10" glyph prints, including an original design—that will only be available during the Kickstarter and never again—will be available via the campaign. In other words, if you want a patch, get it now, or be prepared to wait a few months!
> 
> We are also pleased to release several prints of Gauger’s non-Glyph illustration, [True Love Waits (the Virgins)](https://www.etsy.com/listing/278043258/true-love-waits-the-virgins-giclee-13-x?ref=shop_home_active_1). These prints are part of a closed edition of 10 and were recently exhibited at the Seattle Erotic Art Festival. This is a closed edition of 10 prints in the purple/pink/blue colorway  of “True Love Waits (The Virgins)”, a digital illustration by Eliza Gauger. “Closed edition” means that once these prints are sold, no more will be produced. Each print is signed in gold ink by the artist, and numbered in pencil on the back.The giclée is 13 x 19" (33.02 x 48.26cm), printed on Epson velvet acid-free archival and museum-quality 100% cotton rag, which will preserve its jewel colors, deep blacks and brilliant whites for decades. A [single framed print](https://www.etsy.com/listing/278014200/true-love-waits-the-virgins-giclee?ref=shop_home_feat_4) from this series is also currently available.
