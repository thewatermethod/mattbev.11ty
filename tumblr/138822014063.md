> Here are some mushroom people for Dungeon World. But be careful. Some of them are mean.  
>   
> Fungoid                                               Amorphous, Horde, Organized  
> Spear (d6 damage)                                      6 HP 1 Armor  
> Close, Reach  
> Special Qualities: Telepathy                                                                                                    
> Weird things live in the moldy caverns of the Hollow Earth. Take fungoids, for example. They’re a race of vaguely humanoid mushroom creatures that form colonies within damp, dark places like abandoned mines and dungeons. They have spongy bodies, a thick cap in place of a head, and they lack a nose or mouth. They’re able to eject psychoactive spores that allow them to communicate telepathically with other creatures and as a defensive measure, can also cause wild hallucinations or debilitating nausea. They reproduce quickly and when conditions are ideal, a cluster of fungoids can grow into a full-blown infestation in a matter of weeks. While not inherently evil, fungoids are often feared because their presence inevitably insures the spreading of toxic molds and other malignant forms of fungus life. Instinct: To protect the colony  
> • Hurl a spear  
> • Spread debilitating spores  
> • Retreat and regroup
> 
> Psychedelic Fungoid                                               Amorphous, Group, Intelligent, Magical, Organized  
> Mental Blast (d8 damage, ignores armor)                                      9 HP 1 Armor  
> Near, Far, Forceful  
> Special Qualities: Telekinesis, Telepathy                                                                                                    
> Fungoids growing beneath a wizard’s keep or other highly magical area sometimes develop strange powers. While all fungoids are somewhat psychic, psychedelic individuals gain an advanced intelligence, extrasensory perception, and even the ability to move objects with their minds. The spores produced by psychedelic fungoids are highly potent and they are quick to use them, causing intense hallucinations that can last for days. They enjoy watching their victims react to such an altered state.                     Instinct: To alter the minds of others  
> • Cause intense hallucinations  
> • Use telekinesis to hurl something or someone  
> • Send a telepathic message
> 
> Death Cap Fungoid                                               Amorphous, Group, Organized  
> Rotting Touch (d8+2 damage, ignores armor)                                      9 HP 1 Armor  
> Close, Messy  
> Special Qualities: Telepathy                                                                                                    
> Areas with high concentrations of undead sometimes spawn an especially toxic breed of albino fungoid. They have a deadly touch that causes flesh to rot rapidly, reducing a creature as large as a horse to nothing but a pile of wet bones in minutes. They’re exceptionally cruel and often collect the bones of past victims. Instinct: To spread decay flesh  
> • Spread sickening spores  
> • Rot off a body part  
> • Call on zombie or ghoul allies            
> Humongous Fungoid                                               Amorphous, Huge, Organized, Solitary  
> Smash (d10+5 damage)                                      23 HP 3 Armor  
> Forceful, Reach  
> Special Qualities: Telepathy                                                                                                    
> When a huge creature like a giant or dragon dies in a dark and humid place, an exceptionally massive fungoid cluster can grow from the corpse. Instead of splitting into multiple individuals, sometimes the cluster can meld together and form one gigantic walking colony. These titanic creatures serve as beasts of burden and bodyguards to their smaller brethren, smashing would-be predators into jelly with their gargantuan fists. Instinct: To protect the colony  
> • Hurl a boulder  
> • Carry an ally out of harm’s way            
> • Pulverize!
