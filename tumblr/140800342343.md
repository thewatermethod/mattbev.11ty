> > The history of surfing is, in many ways, the history of innovations in surfboard design. Jim Heimann, an executive editor at Taschen Books, pays special attention to the board in _Surfing_ , a gorgeous—and massive—clothbound book that could easily become the new bible for the sport. The book provides, among other things, a visual guide to the surfboard’s evolution, from rudimentary planks of wood to modern-day creations that combine materials like fiberglass with polyurethane foam.
