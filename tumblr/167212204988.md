> KINDS OF IMAGES DUE 9/7

> Edward Gorey, [Haunted New Orleans](http://catalogue.swanngalleries.com/asp/fullCatalogue.asp?salelot=2403++++++57+&refno=++709922&saletype=), pen and ink with wash, published in Holiday Magazine June 1961.
>
