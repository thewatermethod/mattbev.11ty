I’m at a child’s birthday party writing this on the new improved iOS where it’s anyone’s guess if the next letter you type will be capitalized or not. So pardon any capitalization mistakes or factual inaccuracies.

Wizywig is the story of very early computer hacker and also the story of the way computers have changed our culture. I work in IT, and it never ceases to amaze me that for all the time people spend on computers, they have so little curiosity for what’s going on even at the most basic level past the surface.

It could be cultural - that seems to be central to this book. The fear of the hacker etc, these are evening news buzz words. How many people even have the first idea what a hacker actually does? I mean, literally, what does the hacker do?

Wizywig is the story of a young man with admittedly criminal tendencies punished in great excess to his crime, because people were afraid of what he knew and what he could do. It is well illustrated and cleverly structured. It rarely descends into cliche.
