> John Carpenter’s Escape from New York had a North American One-Sheet that was illustrated by Chuck Foxen.
> 
> Escape from New York is one of the best action flicks you will ever see. Period. I have heard rumors of it being remade on a couple of occasions, and I honestly hope they just leave it alone. the movie is so good, it really does not need anything done to it. the special effects and computer imagery look so outdated that they actually kinda work as science fiction noir at this point.
> 
> besides, how the hell can you recast Kurt Russell, Lee Van Cleef, Ernest Borgnine, Harry Dean Stanton, Adrienne Barbeau, and Isaac Hayes?!
> 
> Isaac Hayes played the Duke. a badass in charge of the entirety of New York City. He rode around NYC in a Cadillac Limo with Chandeliers for headlights, Curb Feelers, and he wore a Civil-War Era Captain’s Jacket. You cannot recast that role. Seriously.
> 
> Hollywood: leave this piece of Americana alone. Please.
> 
> and if you have never seen this flick… change that.
