> Amazing Spider-Man, Vol. 1 # 33 Pages 01-05, Plotted and Illustrated by Steve Ditko, with Letters and Bordering by Artie Simek, and Stan Lee both wrote the Script/Dialogue and did the Editing.
> 
> these 5 pages represent the most iconic Spider-Man story of all time. everything you need to know about the character comes through in the first 5 pages of a 22 page comic book. think about that.
> 
> Ditko left Marvel shortly after this issue hit the stands, with nary a look-back since. I once saw an article written by a guy that went to Ditko’s home to interview him about his comics work. in the corner of Ditko’s office was a PILE of original artwork from the Silver Age up until current. a pile that was more than a foot high, casually stacked underneath a window sill, and in no discernible order. the interviewer asked Steve if he could look at the pages on the floor, and Steve said, “No”. Steve only wanted to talk about his current work. after all, Steve created Spider-Man about 50 years ago, when he was in his 30s.
>
