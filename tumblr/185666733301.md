> SAṂSĀRA \[aka SANGSĀRA\]  
> 
> _\[noun\]_
> 
> the cycle of life, death and rebirth to which life in the material world is bound (reincarnation); to wander through states of existence.
> 
> _Etymology_: from Sanskrit _Saṃsāra_, _संसार._
> 
> _\-_
> 
> **Original:** Buddhist Thangka Bhavacakra Samsara Wheel of Life Brocade Scroll
