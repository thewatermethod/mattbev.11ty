> CORONA
> 
> _\[noun\]_
> 
> 1\. a white or coloured circle or set of concentric circles of light seen around a luminous body, especially around the sun or moon.
> 
> 2\. _Meteorology_: such a circle or set of circles having a small radius and ranging in colour from blue inside to red outside, attributable to the diffraction caused by thin clouds, mist, or sometimes dust. 
> 
> 3\. also called **_[aureola](http://victoriousvocabulary.tumblr.com/post/21710561475/aureole-noun-1-a-radiance-surrounding-the)_**, **_[aureole](http://victoriousvocabulary.tumblr.com/post/14149672680/aureole-noun-1-a-radiance-surrounding-the-head)_** – _Astronomy_: a faintly luminous envelope outside of the sun’s chromosphere, the inner part consisting of highly ionised elements.
> 
> 4\. a long, straight, untapered cigar, rounded at the closed end.
> 
> 5\. _Botany_: a crownlike appendage, especially one on the inner side of a corolla, as in the narcissus.
> 
> 6\. _Anatomy_: the upper portion or crown of a part, as of the head.
> 
> 7\. _Electricity_: corona discharge.
> 
> _Etymology_: from Latin _corona_, “garland, crown” < Greek _korṓnē_, “crown, curved object”; akin to _korōnís,_ “curved, beaked”, _kórax, “_crow”.
>
