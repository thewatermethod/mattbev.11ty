> Created in 1942, the Heap was a spirit of a German aviator who returned in the form of a nearly mindless green mass of vegetable matter in a swamp. The Heap was savage and wanted to be left alone. He was a super-popular character in his time, and had many descendants and imitators, notably Swamp-Thing, Marvel’s Man-Thing (who borrowed the red-eyes and trunk), and the Incredible Hulk, who is basically the Heap mingled with a Jeckyll/Hyde concept.
