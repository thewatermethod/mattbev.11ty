> 
> The Five Senses is a set of allegorical paintings created at Antwerp in 1617–18 by Jan Brueghel the Elder and Peter Paul Rubens, with Brueghel being responsible for the settings and Rubens for the figures. They are now in the Prado Museum in Madrid.
> 
> Jan Brueghel (1568/9-1625) was a Flemish baroque painter, son of Pieter Bruegel the Elder and father of Jan Brueghel the Younger.
> 
> Peter Paul Rubens, (born June 28, 1577, Siegen, Nassau, Westphalia \[Germany\]—died May 30, 1640, Antwerp, Spanish Netherlands \[now in Belgium\]) was a Flemish Baroque painter.
