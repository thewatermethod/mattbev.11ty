SO,

Google has come out with a new browser called CHROME. Nearly every RSS feed in my reader (Google Reader, incidentally) has linked to the Scott McCloud comic that details the philosophy behind the design.

I’ll probably give it a try at some point, but the whole thing makes me nervous. Google, in the comic, seem very much willing to keep the whole project free and open to anyone to modify, but if I switch to Chrome full time, then I will be using Google products for not only word processing, searching the web, reading my feeds, personal organization, but also accessing all that information. The whole thing is a bit frightening. I am very happy with Firefox 3, so I don’t see making a permanent change, but Google has a history of great products and if anyone could make me switch, it’s likely them.

Wait until they come out with their cell-phones: am I going to be talking on a Google Android phone soon?
