> another reason (and yes, there’s more) that _the wicker man_ is the best and only film ever made is that the cast was full of actors perfectly willing to be both utterly meticulous and absolutely bananas. for example: edward woodward having his uniform taken in just too tight to make howie look repressed and uptight, and refusing to see the completed wicker structure until the filming of the final scene so that his reaction was genuine. christopher lee finally being allowed to play A Role That Wasn’t Count Fucking Dracula, doing the role for free, and subsequently going nuts i.e. wearing his own hunting jacket (it seems), making up his own gay little teaser dance for the procession, and waxing absolutely lord summerisle levels of lyrical in any related interview. ingrid pitt playing her character as a nymphomaniac even though she didn’t even get a name and she only had about six lines. paul giovanni getting the band to smoke weed to get into the mood to record the music but none of them being able to do it because they were lying about giggling. the woman who was sobbing nude on the gravestone being on her period and therefore just bleeding on the grass of the grave. thank you all for going insane circa october 1972
