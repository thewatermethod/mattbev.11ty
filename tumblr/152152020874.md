> It’s a new edit with new layout and new art. I’m not sure if I’m going to back it or not. I absolutely don’t NEED this book.

> But I do WANT it. The layout of the other two leave a little to be desired. They’re not bad, they just don’t have a specific feel to it. It used a lot of older Necromancer Games art work, and some of the new original art in it isn’t very good. It doesn’t have a cohesive feel. This new one seems like it does. I am excited about it. I feel like why not get it. I can always sell the other ones (and I have a feeling the Erol Otus one will be become a collectors item).
