> October viewing continued tonight with one of my favorites, _The Black Cat_ (1934).  I’ve seen this film about 10 times and I have to say it is still my favorite of the Universal Films of the 1930′s.  I still pick up on things that are new to me.  The direction is perfect.  Bela and Boris are great.  Yes, David Manners is still David Manners, but somehow it all works extremely well.  
> 
> Boris says it best, “_The Black Cat_ does not die.  _The Black Cat_ is deathless.  As deathless as evil.”
> 
> My thoughts exactly on this outstanding film.
