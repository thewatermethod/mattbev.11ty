[The Encyclopedia of Early Earth](http://www.amazon.com/The-Encyclopedia-Early-Earth-Novel/dp/0316225819) by [Isabel Greenberg](http://www.isabelnecessary.com/)

A beautifully rendered, imaginative story.An evocative and sweet fairy tale that never becomes twee or Disneyish.

The story concerns the journey of a young storyteller across the ancient world. He shares stories with Viking and Persian analogues, frequently running afoul of an omnipresent bird headed god and his children.

The cosmology may be rooted in some actual traditional stories, but I suspect only loosely so. The cultures were inventively decorated with myths.

I found this book captivating.
