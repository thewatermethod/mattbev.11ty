stubbom asked:

> Out of interest, who is the most beautiful woman in art history in your opinion?

[femalebeautyinart](https://femalebeautyinart.tumblr.com/post/140080659351/out-of-interest-who-is-the-most-beautiful-woman):

> This is exactly the kind of question I love! First up at the plate is Emma Hart (later Hamilton), my icon and dream girl:
> 
> ![image](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/33f556e3f6a686fc0ae4fb967ebc0a32385063d8a87ef924d0556a0c3a265d5b.jpg)
> 
> (_Sketch of Emma Hart as Circe_ by George Romney, 1782)
> 
> Bouguereau’s work is also up there, especially this one:
> 
> ![image](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/8e30ebd2c5e6317df9f00c2e75bb5195457c7c5bbdc34884cfd57a60d7d29fba.jpg)
> 
> (_The Wave_ by William Bouguereau, 1896)
> 
> I love this sculpture by Antonio Corradini, partly because it is _insane_ to me that you can make marble look like that, and also because…I mean, that body:
> 
> ![image](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/63f0d9207f8590557414c944407a9826b0ffa285a628fe3647b28f993aeaa659.jpg)
> 
> (_Veiled Truth_ by Antonio Corradini, c. 1750)
> 
> And Luis Ricardo Falero’s witches also deserve a mention. I like a girl who looks like she could eat me alive:
> 
> ![image](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/8f9c3c8a470a9ec1dbf60b9a7672913f96d9999054f629569a4cc8b8505354c3.jpg)
> 
> (_Vision of Faust_ by Luis Ricardo Falero, 1878)
> 
> But yeah, gun to my head I’d go with Emma Hart every time.
