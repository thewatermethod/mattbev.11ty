> > today was supposed to be a fun tabletop day.
> > 
> > i’d signed up to play monsterhearts and d&d at a local gaming store, in the afternoon and evening respectively. but when i showed up for the afternoon game, i was intercepted by the store’s owner. they said they’d discussed this with me before, and that dogs were not allowed at store events. and they were right. we had discussed it before.
> > 
> > i played in a 6-week campaign at their store last year. preceding the 3rd session, the store sent out an e-mail saying not to bring pets to events. ripley had been coming with me to games, and it was evident this general announcement was in specific reference to us. unfortunately, i only saw the e-mail once i had already arrived. i was embarrassed and worried it would appear to the store and my fellow players that i had deliberately ignored the e-mail. i asked to speak with the owner and apologized. i also clarified that ripley is not a pet. he’s an emotional support animal. when i asked if an exception could be made for service animals, the owner said they would prefer ripley be left at home, but that an exception could be made.
> > 
> > this left me feeling conflicted. i wanted to bring ripley to events, and if i couldn’t bring him, honestly, i preferred not to go at all. though they said an exception could be made, knowing that the owner didn’t want ripley there, i felt uncomfortable bringing him. so the day before the next session i called the store and spoke to an employee to clarify the store’s position. the employee put me on hold, spoke with the owner, and gave me the same answer i received in-person. i explained why this reply was insufficient and asked for an up-or-down decision: either ripley was allowed or he wasn’t. i was put on hold again. when the employee returned, i was told ripley’s presence was unequivocally permitted. the rest of the campaign proceeded without incident.  
> > 
> > so it came as a surprise today when, as i arrived to play monsterhearts, i was confronted by the owner. they behaved as if our previous interactions hadn’t occurred. instead, their recollection was that i’d been told ripley wasn’t allowed. end of discussion. they couldn’t allow some dogs and not others, service animal or no. people with allergies and other conditions had to be taken into account also. i tried to share my recollection, but it was denied. i also mentioned that i had played d&d there with ripley 2 weeks prior on free rpg day. his presence wasn’t a problem then. they replied that his presence actually had been a problem. ripley could attend today’s events, but would not be allowed henceforth. unfortunately, i didn’t feel like playing after this discussion. i felt embarrassed, uncomfortable, and on the verge of tears. the owner offered a refund for the two events. i accepted and left.
> > 
> > i understand that people with allergies deserve consideration. i am allergic to both cats and dogs. i adopted ripley specifically because he does not shed, significantly limiting the release of allergens. i keep him in a carrier on my lap during games. the service he provides is legitimate and, in social environments, necessary. it is upsetting to feel i’ve done everything to avoid a situation like this only to have it occur nonetheless.
