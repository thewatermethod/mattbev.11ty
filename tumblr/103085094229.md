> CACOETHES
> 
> _\[noun\]_
> 
> an uncontrollable urge or desire, especially for something harmful; to perform something inadvisable.
> 
> _Etymology_: from Latin _cacoēthes_, “malignant disease”, from Greek _kakoēthēs_, “of an evil disposition” + _ēthos_, “character”.
>
