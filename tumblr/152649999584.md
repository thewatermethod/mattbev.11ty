> **Signs in Aklo, Reading List  
> **This list is in response [to an excellent question from](http://wilburwhateley.tumblr.com/post/148823346513/i-was-wondering-if-there-are-any-cthulhu-mythos) [@nyaarlathotep](https://tmblr.co/maQnmc6rrLYsDDKpCkGha9A) (with a follow-up question from [@ekhidne](https://tmblr.co/mafttM-eRT8Lp-R7Trfxerg)) quite some time ago concerning Mythos connections to the Fae, as well as general examples of Fae-related Horror. Below is a _very_ small selection of recommended titles—a mix of short fiction, books, and some nonfiction resources. Note that beyond the works by Machen and Blackwood (and a few by HPL, himself), the Fae are not often referred to as such in Mythos fiction. There are some exceptions, but it’s _completely_ reasonable for someone to argue that it is incorrect to refer to the creatures in many of these works as ‘Fae’ at all—regardless of authorial intent, common and/or reasonably supported readings, etc. I can certainly see both perspectives with many of these titles. With that in mind, this is intended to be nothing more than a starting point for those interested. \[Given my tendency to stop and read/re-read everything I stumble upon (ha), if I waited until this list were ‘complete,’ it would never get posted…so here you go.\] \*I apologize for the ‘keep reading,’ mobile users.\*
