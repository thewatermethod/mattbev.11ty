> GRIPULOUS
> 
> _\[adjective\]_
> 
> grasping; greedy.
> 
> _Etymology_: grip (Middle English, from Old English _gripe_, “grasp” and _gripa_, “handful”) + _\-ulous_ (a suffix occurring in adjectives borrowed from Latin, with the meaning “inclined to do, habitually engaging in” the action specified by the initial element).
