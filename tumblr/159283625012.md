> All 9 **soviet space dogs**, top to bottom:
> 
> **[Laika](http://enrique262.tumblr.com/post/131823834920/laika-russian-%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BA%D0%B0-c-1954-november-3-1957)** - Sputnik 2, November 3 1957: First living being to reach space, DIED IN ORBIT.
> 
> **[Belka and Strelka](http://enrique262.tumblr.com/post/129449335505/belka-%D0%B1%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%BA%D0%B0-literally-squirrel-or)** -  Sputnik 5, August 19 1960: First living beings to reach space and return ALIVE.
> 
> **[Pchyolka and Mushka](http://enrique262.tumblr.com/post/132127699970/pchyolka-%D0%BF%D1%87%D1%91%D0%BB%D0%BA%D0%B0-little-bee-and-mushka-%D0%BC%D1%83%D1%88%D0%BA%D0%B0)** - Sputnik 6, December 1 1960: Reached orbit, DIED ON REENTRY after a malfunction activated the capsule’s self-destruct system.
> 
> **[Chernushka](http://enrique262.tumblr.com/post/134822480030/chernushka-%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BD%D1%83%D1%88%D0%BA%D0%B0-blackie-was-the-6th)** - Sputnik 9, 9 March 1961: First dog to return alive without a fellow companion.
> 
> **[Zvyozdochka](http://enrique262.tumblr.com/post/134830933425/zvyozdochka-zvezdochka-%D0%B7%D0%B2%D1%91%D0%B7%D0%B4%D0%BE%D1%87%D0%BA%D0%B0-or-starlet)** - Sputnik 10, 25 March 1961: Last dog to go into orbit before the first human, Yuri Gagarin, did, she was also named by him.  
> 
> **[Veterok and Ugolyok](http://enrique262.tumblr.com/post/133703910860/veterok-%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BA-light-breeze-and-ugolyok)** - Cosmos 110, 22 February 1966: Lasting 22 days in orbit, it became the longest space flight by dogs, they were also the last soviet space dogs.
