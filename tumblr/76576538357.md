I don’t think I have time this week to review a book properly, so I’m going to quickly write about a few books that I’ve read recently.

![image](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/93f5f5f3f97852abc3f4dd1ce953fd29cf79a8e85b12da8059d1a7ed3754149f.jpg)

[Qelong by Kenneth Hite](http://www.lotfp.com/store/index.php?route=product/product&product_id=169)

A dark southeast Asian-style OSRPG setting primarily inspired by **Apocalypse Now**, or at least that’s how it reads. Fairly true to the rest of the books published by the Lamentations of the Fire Princess people(guy?). That is to say, grim, unforgiving and tinged with horror. I really enjoy reading RPG setting and scenario books as sort of meta/inter textual-novels. As far as that goes, this book is OK, but I’d recommend [The Magnificent Joop Von Ooms](http://www.lotfp.com/store/index.php?route=product/product&path=36&product_id=158) from the same company/in the same vein or the [Trail of Cthulhu sourcebook](http://www.pelgranepress.com/site/?p=242) by the same author  more highly.

![image](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/7e989695ed77931c040ea41f37b9be07228d02c7e0115a60f689b62136b38c68.jpg)

[Conan vol 14: The Death](http://www.darkhorse.com/Books/22-285/Conan-Volume-14-The-Death-TPB)

The first of the Dark Horse Conan comics I’ve read in awhile, I bought this mainly for the Becky Cloonan art, and the art is consistently great throughout (Cloonan only provides art in the initial chapters of the book, after that Vasilis Lolos takes over and he does a great job). That is about the only reason to read this.

![image](https://thewatermethod.com/tumblr/thewatermethod.tumblr.com/media/7a8102b70ef678ad6461b1261d71ce1ea9cb2432dc0361db7efbabda68a4c5cb.jpg)

[Prophet Vol 1 & 2](http://www.amazon.com/s/ref=nb_sb_ss_c_0_14?url=search-alias%3Dstripbooks&field-keywords=prophet%20brandon%20graham&sprefix=prophet+brando%2Caps%2C301) 

Man these are great. Weird, European style sci-fi art and absolutely bat shit craziness throughout. Exciting, feverish half-remembered 15 to a polybag Youngblood comics from 1995. Highly recommended, if that’s your sort of thing.
