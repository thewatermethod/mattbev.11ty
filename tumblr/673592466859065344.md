> Austrian photographer [Inge Prader](http://www.prader.at/) recently recreated [Gustav Klimt’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Klimt) masterworks for [Style Bible](http://www.stylebible.org/), a part of the [Life Ball Charity Event](http://lifeball.org/en/life-ball/) in Vienna, Austria.  A team of over 50 professionals worked on the demanding photography project which raises funds to help those with HIV/AIDS.  Makeup artists, costume designers, set designers, lighting specialists and many others worked with models and fully ornamented props to bring to life the fascinating, erotically charged work that Klimt is known for during his “Golden Phase.”
