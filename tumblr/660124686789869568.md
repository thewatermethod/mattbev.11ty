> Siouxsie Sioux in photographer Jill Furmanovsky’s Kentish Town studio, November 14, 1983, my edit of original via rockarchive.com.
> 
> “A session with Siouxsie and the Banshees tested every bit of my studio experience. It was only a portrait for a magazine but I wanted to try something more ambitious than a mug shot. Siouxsie has a statuesque quality so my assistant and I built her a very kitsch Grecian set using columns borrowed from the plasterer’s yard below us. 
> 
> I left a message with her record company telling her my idea and requesting that she bring some appropriate clothes. The message never got through to Siouxsie and she arrived in her street clothes which were clearly unsuitable. 
> 
> In a panic I searched around the studio for alternatives while the journalist taped her interview. The solution to the problem was right in front of me - a curtain that divided the changing area from the studio. Wrapping the cloth around myself I got my assistant to take a Polaroid of me which I presented to Siouxsie. She laughed - it was a funny night - but the shot was in the bag.”
