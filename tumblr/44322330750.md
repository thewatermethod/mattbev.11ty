[How To Program Independent Games](http://the-witness.net/news/2011/06/how-to-program-independent-games/)

A presentation by Jonathan Blow, on the topic above.

…

My grandfather is the person who has probably taught me the most about things like plumbing, electrical work, basic construction. He has a name (and great disdain) for those who employ shoddy workmanship, those who bash out a solution quickly in order to get things functional. He calls them ‘hackers.’

Spoiler Alert: In this presentation, Blow sort of advocates the same approach for programming. That is, he directly points out that your programming should not only be optimized to the standards of the CS community, but also to optimize the time spent programming.

Personally, I am but an amateur, but I couldn’t agree more.
