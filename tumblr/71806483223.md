I meant to write more about stuff on Tumblr this previous year. I’ve always wanted to start blogging more prolifically. I rather pathetically managed to squeeze out a few hundred reblogs and maybe 3 original posts. Very sad.

I kept a .txt with all my favorite stuff of the year in it. Here is is.

(An aside: this stuff did not have to come out this year. I just have to have read/watched it/_whatever_ this year)

[The Lathe of Heaven](http://www.amazon.com/The-Lathe-Of-Heaven-Novel/dp/1416556966/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1388542866&sr=8-1&keywords=lathe+of+heaven) and [The Dispossessed](http://www.amazon.com/Dispossessed-Novel-Perennial-Classics/dp/006051275X/ref=tmm_pap_swatch_0?_encoding=UTF8&sr=8-2&qid=1388542890) by Ursula K Leguin.

[A Pretty Mouth](http://www.amazon.com/A-Pretty-Mouth-Molly-Tanzer/dp/1621050505/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1388542918&sr=8-1&keywords=A+Pretty+Mouth), by Molly Tanzer

[The Invisibles Omnibus](http://www.amazon.com/The-Invisibles-Various/dp/1401234593/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1388542936&sr=8-1&keywords=invisibles+omnibus), Grant Morrison

[Boxers/Saints](http://www.amazon.com/Boxers-Saints-Boxed-Gene-Luen/dp/1596439246/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1388542953&sr=8-1&keywords=boxers+saints), by Gene Yang
