> Continuing off from the [“angel halos are demon horns”](http://otherwindow.tumblr.com/post/172299988230/otherwindow-toloveviceforitself) post: 
> 
> **(Angel)** The angel depicted here is a Guardian Angel of the Third Sphere category, hence the three circles. These angels can grow a reflective, porcelain-like layer on the skin.
> 
> * * *
> 
> **(Demon)** If an Angel chooses to leave heaven, they must break their halos into horns by falling to Earth. Mistaken for fallen stars, these “demons” cover themselves with ash, hiding in the dark to avoid being found by angels wishing to bring them back to heaven.
> 
> Different angels rebel for different reasons: Some like Lucifer rebelled for power, while the Guardian Angel above chose independence.
> 
> * * *
> 
> **(Nephilim)** For angels transitioning to human life, they may rip off their now weakened halos (dubbed horns) to fit in with humanity. Called “Nephilim”, these angels disguise themselves as beggars to observe humanity, preparing themselves for mortal life.
> 
> At any point during the Nephilim stage, they may regrow their horns to be demons or angels once more.
> 
> * * *
> 
> **(Human)** The angel/demon depicted above is now a human, but still retains the power of miracles due to their celestial origin. Guardian Angels like the one above has chosen to be a “Shepherd”; protecting humans from the paranormal as they always have, but this time unrestricted by heaven’s orders.
