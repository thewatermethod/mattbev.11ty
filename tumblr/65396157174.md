Like many Argento films, the acting is hammy, the plot is thin (even being an adaptation of Dracula), but the music is great (not Goblin, but there is plenty of theremin and some great callbacks to the Universal classics score) and the colors are vibrant and incredible. I’ve never seen an Argento movie so awash in CG,not being familiar with much of his stuff from the last decade, and I can’t say it’s an improvement.

The script and the performers plants tongue firmly in cheek. There is enough over the top violence and nudity to make Gary Oldman blush.

In the end, though, Argento’s compositional skill is still absolutely incredible and it is still Dracula, so it gets at least a bit of a free pass from me. I think most criticism of this movie stems from unfair expectations, frankly.
