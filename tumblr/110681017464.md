> **The Art of Daniel R. Horne**
> 
> > _Daniel R. Horne has been making art professionally for the past thirty one  years._
> > 
> > _From paperback covers and children’s books to gallery paintings and one of a kind art dolls, Daniel’s art is in many private collections from Hong Kong to New York City._
> > 
> > _Daniel’s art has been featured in many of the genre’s leading magazines and has been honored to have his painting: ARCADIA on the cover of : SPECTRUM the best of fantastic art., The covers  for MONSTERS FROM THE VAULT, SCREEM MAGAZINE and a fine interview in make up artist magazine._
> > 
> > _Daniel’s classic monster paintings are in the collection of DAVID and JAN HAFT, GUILLERMO DEL TORO, RICK BAKER, GREG NICOTERO ._
> > 
> > _No matter the medium, Daniel gives each and every one of his creations life and a history._
