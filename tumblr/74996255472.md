Black is the Color by Julia Gfrorer is the collected edition of a webcomic, which I never read. So as far as I’m concerned, this is an original graphic novel, were I to use the parlance of our times.

Gfrorer, whose name actually includes an umlaut over the O, is a graphic artist of some talent. Her line is delicate and illustrative and perhaps wrongly reminds me of woodcuts and illuminated manuscripts and things like that. The art is definitely the strongest part of the comic.

I would be interested to see a version with an additional color or tone added in. Something like the effect that she employs on the cover, perhaps, the mixture of pencil and pen. I’m also thinking of the color scheming employed in something like Barbarella comics. (Black and white plus one)

The story is the kind of thing that I personally enjoy but some may find too light on plot. A sailor is set adrift to die. He has an impressionistic love affair with a mermaid. He dies. Death and sexuality and the relationship therein are examined. 

The book has one of the best titles of a book I’ve read in a long time and the cover typography is super cool.

It’s basically the most heavy metal comic I’ve read in, well, perhaps ever.
