> Barnes Foundation (Philadelphia), Collection Gallery, Room 21, South Wall\\n
> 
> In 1917 Leopold Zborowski, a Polish-born poet-turned-art dealer, provided the impoverished Amedeo Modigliani with a space in his apartment in which to work, as well as art supplies, models, and a daily stipend. While ensconced in Zborowski’s home and at his request, Modigliani embarked on an ambitious series of just over two dozen female nudes.
> 
> Judith Dolkart, _The Barnes Foundation: Masterworks_
> 
>   
> _Medium: Oil on canvas_
