> On May 16th, 2016, the first official Problem Glyphs art book will launch on Kickstarter. This book collects one hundred glyphs and their associated problems, and features an introduction by Eliza Gauger and a foreword by award-winning writer Warren Ellis.   
> 
> _Problem Glyphs_ will be a premium edition and display-worthy art book featuring a Smyth sewn genuine clothbound hard cover with gold foil-stamped cover illustrations. The interior will be printed on beautiful matte coated art paper. Created in collaboration with [Strix Publishing](http://strixpublishing.com/), this book will showcase Gauger’s work on Problem Glyphs in an edition worthy of space on your coffee table or book shelf.
> 
> The Kickstarter campaign will offer backers not only a chance to support Gauger and purchase the book, but also to receive additional rewards including an exclusive new art print available only through the campaign. Other rewards are being finalized but will likely include a numbered Special Edition of the book bound in leather and signed by Gauger, limited to less than one hundred copies. The book and all rewards are expected to be delivered to backers before the end of 2016.
> 
> More information will be released about this book in the coming weeks. Please stay tuned to Problem Glyphs on Tumblr and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/problemglyphs/), as well as Strix Publishing, and Eliza Gauger’s personal [Twitter](https://twitter.com/3liza).
