> **\[+ + SQUIRE OF CHAOS + +\]**
> 
> _“The Lord of those illusory lands was given to collecting precious poisons - he wore a haze of fever about himself, and to him the Monarch bequeathed a holy toxin. Ignoring the warnings of his priestly attendants he took the new colour of the venom within himself. Turning to face his court, he declared - “No Lord, I, But A Squire, With A Perfect Chaos In My Belly”. Upon hearing this, his men rejoiced, and his name was taken into their own flesh that it could not be stricken easily”_
