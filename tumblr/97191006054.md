I just listened to the Audiobook of Tim Powers novel ON STRANGER TIDES. I really enjoyed it. It is a lovely mix of historical fancy, historical fiction, and Weird stuff.

The plot is labyrinthine, just like an 18th century adventure novel, complete with long lost relatives, family betrayals, mistaken identities, and reborn Pirate Kings. There’s vodoun, humor, and gore.

The audiobook was compellingly recorded by Bronson Pinchot. I could listen him to doing “pirate voice” all day.

Sorry to gush like this, I read a lot of technical stuff and non-fiction and when I read a good old fashioned fun novel like this one, and it is really actually good, I get as excited as a small child.
