> PERVIGILIUM
> 
> _\[noun\]_
> 
> 1\. a watching all night; a vigil; a night vigil.
> 
> 2. _Pathology_: disinclination to sleep; wakefulness.
> 
> 3\. a nocturnal festival in honour of a god, especially the _Bona Dea_.
> 
> _Etymology_: from Latin, “the vigil”, “a devotional watch”.
