> **Career Timeline: 1976 - _Dune_**
> 
> Moebius meets Alejandro Jodorwosky, and begins work as a concept artist on a filma adaptation of Frank Herbert’s _Dune_, specializing in costume design and storyboarding. The film was infamously abandoned, but created many new relationships for Moebius, particularly with Jodorowsky and writer Dan O’Bannon.
