> Another animated illustration for [Wired Italia](http://www.wired.it/ "Wired Italia"). This accompanied a column about AI, specifically the question of how or if an intelligent machine could be told apart from a human. The text references the [scene early in Blade Runner](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HN9Znt4HssU) where a suspected replicant is questioned using the [Voight-Kampff device](http://bladerunner.wikia.com/wiki/Voight-Kampff_machine) so I drew inspiration from that, hopefully not too directly!
